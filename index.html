What are cells?

All living organisms are made of cells — the basic building blocks of life. Scientists use microscopes to see them because cells are tiny!

Inside each cell are special parts called organelles (say: or-guh-NELS). Each organelle has a job, just like organs in your body.

Plant and animal cells are both eukaryotic — they have a true nucleus and membrane-bound organelles. Some structures are only in animal cells, and many are found in both!

Plant Cell vs. Animal Cell

🌿 Plant Cell Only

  • 🧱
    Cell wall — rigid wall of cellulose for support & protection
  • ☀️
    Chloroplasts — do photosynthesis (light → glucose); contain chlorophyll; found in leaves & green parts
  • 💧
    Large central vacuole — stores water & nutrients; creates turgor pressure for firmness & support

🐾 Animal Cell Typically

  • 🫧
    Small vacuoles — several small ones instead of one giant vacuole
  • ♻️
    Lysosomes — recycling centers; enzymes break down waste & worn-out parts
  • 🧵
    Centrioles — help make spindle fibers that pull chromosomes apart during cell division; usually in pairs

🤝 Found in BOTH plant & animal cells

Cell membrane · Cytoplasm · Nucleus · Nucleolus · Ribosomes · Mitochondria · Endoplasmic reticulum (rough & smooth) · Golgi apparatus
