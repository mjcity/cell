⚠️ Watch Out, Richard!

Ribosome ≠ Lysosome! On the practice sheet the question "Which organelle makes proteins?" was answered Lysosome. The right answer is Ribosome. Trick: RIBOsome = Robot factory that Builds. LYSOsome = Litter / trash can that breaks things down.

Spell these right: mitochondria (mi-toe-KON-dree-uh) • ribosomes • nucleus (one) / nuclei (many) • eukaryotic (YOU-carry-otic: "YOU have a nucleus") • prokaryotic ("PRO = NO nucleus").

Don't leave blanks! #3 (eukaryotic) was blank — if stuck, use the word bank and cross off words you already used.

Mitochondria vs chloroplast: Mitochondria = battery that releases energy from sugar. Chloroplast = sugar jar that makes sugar from sunlight.
